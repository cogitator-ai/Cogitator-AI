Channels: a Microsoft Teams channel on the Bot Framework protocol, with no SDK.

- `teamsChannel({ appId, appPassword, tenantId })` takes activities on a webhook, on a server of the channel's own (`/api/messages`, bodies up to 1 MB, other routes such as a GitHub webhook served on the same port) or through `handleWebhook` and `webhookListener()`. Every request must carry a Bot Framework JWT, checked with `node:crypto` alone: RS256 only, signed by a key of the published JWKS (cached, loaded again once a day, and for an unknown key at most every five minutes), issued by `https://api.botframework.com` for the App ID, valid now within five minutes of skew, with a `serviceurl` claim equal to the activity's `serviceUrl` and a key endorsed for `msteams`. Activities are answered at once and handled after, each once, and the bot's own are skipped.
- Answers go to the Bot Connector of the conversation's verified `serviceUrl` (optionally limited to `serviceUrlHosts`), with a client credentials token from the bot's own tenant (`appType: 'SingleTenant'` by default, `'MultiTenant'` for legacy bots, `'UserAssignedMSI'` with a `tokenProvider`), cached until shortly before it expires. Throttled and failing requests (`429`, `412`, `502`, `504`) are tried again with a backoff that doubles, with jitter, requests to one conversation are spaced under Teams' 7 a second, and errors are `TeamsApiError`s with the Bot Connector's `errorCode`, such as `MessageWritesBlocked` when a user blocked the bot.
- Each personal chat, group chat and channel thread is a conversation of its own: `channelId` is the conversation id, a channel thread's root message is its `topicId`, and `userId` is the user's Entra object id. In group chats and channels the bot answers @mentions without the mention in the text (`trigger: 'all'` answers every message an app with resource-specific consent gets), and only users of its own tenant unless `tenants` says otherwise.
- Answers are Markdown with `textFormat: 'extendedmarkdown'`, up to 13653 characters a message (80 KB of JSON), and stream into a message the bot edits. Buttons are Adaptive Cards with `Action.Execute`, answered in the invoke response, and an `Action.Submit` fallback, both delivered to `onAction`, so approval prompts get Approve and Deny buttons. Files upload in personal chats through a file consent card, and elsewhere go out by their public URL. A message the Gateway refuses while it stops is answered `503`.
- `ChannelType` includes `'teams'`, and a channel thread is a `topicId`.
- In `cogitator.yml`, `channels.teams` takes owners (Entra object ids), `appType`, `tenantId`, `tenants`, `users`, `trigger`, `serviceUrlHosts` and the webhook port and path (default `3978`), with the secrets from `TEAMS_APP_ID`, `TEAMS_APP_PASSWORD` and `TEAMS_TENANT_ID`. `cogitator init`, `cogitator wizard` and create-cogitator-app (`--channels teams`) set up a single-tenant bot, serving its messaging endpoint on the WebChat port when WebChat runs, and the GitHub webhook on the Teams port when both run.
